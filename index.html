How the bands work

Bands are top 1–6, middle 7–13, bottom 14–20 — each band pays its top two, which lands the prizes on positions 1, 2, 7, 8, 14 and 20. Every spot on the table is within a few places of money.

Lucky Dip: drawn in May from the names of everyone who won nothing else. That guarantees seven different people get paid rather than one person doubling up. Lucky Dip is a payout to a person, not a table position — it isn't tied to a club's finishing place.

Three things to be aware of

13 of 20 win nothing

That's the cost of going from a full ladder to seven prizes. If that bothers you, split the Lucky Dip into 5 × €50 instead of 1 × €250 — you'd have 11 winners and the draw becomes the most anticipated part of the night.

There are cliff edges

3rd place pays €0 while 7th pays €300. Sixth is the single worst position to finish in this design — narrowly missing the top band and the middle band. People will notice this in May, so it's better to say it out loud in August than defend it later.

The middle band is the fairest money

€500 sits on 7th and 8th, which is the one range genuinely open to any club in the league — Villa, Newcastle, Brighton, Palace, Everton, a good promoted side. The top prizes will follow the big clubs and the Wooden Spoon will follow the 1000/1 shot, but nobody can predict 7th.

Two rules to lock before the draw

Rule 1 Prizes settle on the official adjusted table — points deductions count.

Rule 2 The Lucky Dip is drawn after the final table is known.
