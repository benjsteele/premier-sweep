Home Rules Sweepstake Draw

1. Entry & eligibility

Entry: Invite only. Feel free to share with friends if they're interested.

Number of entries: 20, one per Premier League club.

Entry fee: €100 per player.

Entry deadline: Closed by 20th August.

Payment method: Sheets are virtual, by invite only.

Entries are only confirmed once a sheet is filled and paid. Once the first sheet of 20 is completed, those entries are locked in. If a second sheet of 20 is opened and doesn't fill up, everyone on that second sheet gets their money back.

2. The draw

Draw date: 8pm, Thursday 20th, Regan's Bar.

Draw method: Done by Jim Regan, from a hat.

Each player is randomly assigned one of the 20 Premier League clubs. Results are published on the Sweepstake Draw page. Once made, assignments are final and cannot be swapped or traded.

4. The Lucky Dip

  • Everyone who has not won any of prizes 1–6 goes into the Lucky Dip pool. Winning a table-position prize does not exclude a player from other table-position prizes, but it does exclude them from the Lucky Dip.
  • The Lucky Dip is drawn in May, after the season's final table is confirmed — see Rule 6 below.
  • One name is drawn at random from the non-winners' pool for €250. The Lucky Dip is a single €250 draw — it will not be split into smaller draws.
  • Done by Jim Regan, from a hat.

5. Rules to lock before the draw

Rule 1 Prizes settle on the official adjusted Premier League table — any points deductions handed down during the season count.

Rule 2 The Lucky Dip is drawn only after the final table is known, once all 38 matchweeks are complete and any deductions are finalised.

Rule 3 No swapping of teams. Once the draw is made, assignments are final and cannot be traded.
